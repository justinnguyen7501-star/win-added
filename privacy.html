Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

Win Added ("we", "the site") shows League of Legends item builds ranked by how much each purchase changes a player's chance of winning. This policy explains what information the site uses and why.

Visitors to this site

You don't need an account to use Win Added, and we don't ask for any personal information. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and shows no ads.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you load a page, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address under the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive or store this information ourselves.

League of Legends match data

Our statistics come from ranked match records obtained through the official Riot Games API, under Riot's developer terms. These records include in-game details such as champions, items, runes and timelines, along with Riot's pseudonymous player identifiers.

  • We use match records only to calculate combined statistics across thousands of games.
  • The site shows only those combined statistics. It never shows player names, Riot IDs or any individual player's games.
  • Match records are stored privately on our own computer and deleted after about four weeks.
  • We don't sell, share or publish match records.

Changes

If the site ever adds accounts, ads or analytics, we will update this policy first and change the date above.

Contact

For questions, or to ask us to remove data related to your account, open an issue on the Win Added GitHub page.
