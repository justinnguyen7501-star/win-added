WinAdded
How this works

Win chance added. A model trained on every minute of every game estimates each team's chance to win. When a player finishes an item, we compare their win chance just before the purchase with 10 minutes later. Items that reliably push that number up rank higher. The method builds on xPetu's master's thesis (Aalto University, 2024), which first applied win probability added to League items.

Fair comparison. Players who are already ahead buy items sooner, so every result is adjusted for how far ahead the player was, the game minute, their keystone and their server.

Better / Worse / Too close. The bar shows the range the true effect most likely falls in (95%). Green means the whole range is above zero, red means the whole range is below. Grey means we can't tell it apart from zero yet. Options with fewer than 100 games are marked.

Limits. Items 4 to 6 are compared across all earlier builds, so treat them as a shortlist. Results use the last two weeks of games and refresh every week.
